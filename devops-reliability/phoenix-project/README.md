# The Phoenix Project: A Novel about IT, DevOps, and Helping Your Business Win

This subject turns a business novel into a practical operating model for production IT.
You will follow how local heroics fail, why overloaded work queues collapse delivery,
and how the Three Ways reframe IT as a flow system tied to business outcomes. The
concept order starts with the crisis framing, then moves through constraints, flow,
feedback, and culture-level transformation.

**Source book:** *The Phoenix Project: A Novel about IT, DevOps, and Helping Your Business Win* - Gene Kim, Kevin Behr, George Spafford (IT Revolution, 2013).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`phoenix-project/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `phoenix-project/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: see the system, find the bottleneck, constrain WIP, improve flow, then scale the operating model across the org.
