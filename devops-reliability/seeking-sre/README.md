# Seeking SRE

This subject extends classic SRE into the harder realities of different companies,
team shapes, and human systems. Instead of a single canonical model, it focuses on
adoption paths, social and organizational constraints, and how reliability work changes
as organizations mature. The concept order goes from adaptation choices, to people and
culture, to long-horizon SRE strategy.

**Source book:** *Seeking SRE* - David Blank-Edelman (O'Reilly, 2018).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`seeking-sre/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `seeking-sre/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: start with SRE adoption models, then human and cultural practices, then strategic scaling choices.

**Cross-subject prerequisites** where relevant: `sre/03`, `sre/04`, `sre/08`, and `sre/10` provide the baseline SRE mechanics this subject adapts for diverse organizations.
