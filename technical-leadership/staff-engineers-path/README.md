# The Staff Engineer's Path

This subject focuses on the practical day-to-day work of being a staff engineer.
It follows the three pillars from the book: thinking at the right scope, executing
through others, and leveling up the people and systems around you. The lessons build
from personal operating habits to organization-level technical leadership.

**Source book:** *The Staff Engineer's Path* - Tanya Reilly (O'Reilly, 2022).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`staff-engineers-path/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `staff-engineers-path/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: big-picture thinking first, then execution mechanisms, then multiplier behaviors.
