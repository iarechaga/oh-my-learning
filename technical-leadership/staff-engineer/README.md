# Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track

This subject is about what it actually means to operate at staff-plus level without
becoming a manager. You will learn the common archetypes, the kind of impact that
defines the role, and the operating habits that make senior technical leadership work.
The goal is to turn "become staff" from a vague aspiration into a concrete playbook.

**Source book:** *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track* - Will Larson (Stripe Press, 2021).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`staff-engineer/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `staff-engineer/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: role fundamentals first, then path-to-title, then operating patterns, then long-term staff-plus leverage.
