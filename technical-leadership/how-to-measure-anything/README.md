# How to Measure Anything: Finding the Value of Intangibles in Business

This subject teaches practical measurement for decisions that feel too fuzzy to
quantify. You will learn how to reduce uncertainty with calibrated estimates,
targeted data collection, and value-of-information thinking. The lessons progress
from measurement fundamentals to organization-level decision economics.

**Source book:** *How to Measure Anything: Finding the Value of Intangibles in Business* - Douglas W. Hubbard (Wiley, 2014).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`how-to-measure-anything/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `how-to-measure-anything/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: uncertainty and estimation basics first, then measurement design, then value-of-information and portfolio-level decision use.
