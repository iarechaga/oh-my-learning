# The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change

This subject follows the management ladder in engineering from mentoring and tech
lead work to director, VP, and CTO scope. It focuses on what changes at each level:
your time horizon, your leverage, and the systems you must build to help others
succeed. The sequence is intentionally role-progressive, mirroring real career growth.

**Source book:** *The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change* - Camille Fournier (O'Reilly, 2017).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`managers-path/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `managers-path/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: early management foundations first, then team and org management, then executive leadership.
