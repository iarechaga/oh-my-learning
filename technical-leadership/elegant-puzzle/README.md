# An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management

This subject treats engineering management as a system you can design, observe,
and improve. It covers how organizations scale, how structure shapes outcomes,
and how management decisions compound over time. You will move from team-level
execution to company-level organizational design and leadership systems.

**Source book:** *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management* - Will Larson (Stripe Press, 2019).

**Seniority baseline:** staff (lessons range senior->principal).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`elegant-puzzle/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `elegant-puzzle/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: management systems foundations first, then team design and execution, then org-scale leadership systems.
