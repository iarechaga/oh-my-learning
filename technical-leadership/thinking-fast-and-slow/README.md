# Thinking, Fast and Slow

This subject builds a decision-making toolkit grounded in cognitive science.
It explains how intuitive judgments and deliberate reasoning interact, where
biases and heuristics mislead us, and how to make higher-quality decisions in
uncertain environments. The sequence moves from core mental models to applied
judgment and choice under risk.

**Source book:** *Thinking, Fast and Slow* - Daniel Kahneman (Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2011).

**Seniority baseline:** senior (lessons range mid->senior).

**How to use this subject:** read a lesson on your own, then ask to *discuss
`thinking-fast-and-slow/<NN>`* (e.g. *"discuss `thinking-fast-and-slow/03`"*). Ordered by dependency: core cognition models first, then bias mechanisms, then judgment, risk, and decision frameworks.
